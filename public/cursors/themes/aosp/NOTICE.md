Source: https://github.com/Tech-Tac/aosp-cursors/tree/554015dfeb2692cf2c05c9924dd3d67cd9c15365/vector

Converted Android VectorDrawable paths to SVG, resolved colors to black/white, selected one static wait frame with an outline, and composed progress from the source arrow and wait artwork. Single-direction resize roles use the corresponding original AOSP axis or diagonal artwork. The pointing hand is recolored with a white interior and black outline. Existing AOSP arrow, text and crosshair assets are unchanged. These assets are used only by the AOSP theme.
