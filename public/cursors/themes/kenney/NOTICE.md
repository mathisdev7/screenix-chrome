Original outlined white vector artwork. Paths and colors are preserved. Artwork is centered and uniformly scaled to a 22-unit maximum coordinate envelope in a 32-unit square canvas; the three hand shapes share a scale so a closed hand retains its smaller size. SVG dimensions are 256 by 256. Hotspots are assigned at the arrow/finger/pen tip, magnifier center, hand palm, or symbol center, and transformed with the artwork. Single-direction resizing uses the pack's corresponding axis or diagonal cursor. Drag-ask uses help and all-resize uses the four-way resize cursor. No artwork from another theme is used.
