Upstream SVG geometry and palette are retained for supplied desktop roles. The pointing hand is recolored white with a black outline. Screenix adaptations extend Mint to missing desktop roles using its own arrow, hand, move geometry and palette; these additions are not claimed as original upstream assets and use no other cursor theme artwork. Screenix additions in this directory are also dedicated under CC0-1.0. The unused attack, loot and talk game cursors are not mapped to unrelated desktop roles.

Artwork uses a 32-unit square canvas at 256×256 SVG dimensions, normalized to a maximum 22-unit coordinate envelope including stroke padding. Hands share their scale so the closed hand remains smaller. Hotspots follow the transformed visible tip or symbol center; the upstream hand title hotspot lies outside its finger and is corrected. Resizing uses the corresponding axis or diagonal design. Wait/progress use static artwork.
