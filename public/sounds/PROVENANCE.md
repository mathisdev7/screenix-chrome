# Subtle v1

Original synthesized transients created for Screenix. No recordings or third-party samples. Deterministic noise and damped sinusoidal components, mono 48 kHz PCM16. These assets are dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Listening and packaged playback validation remain user-run release checks.

The optional Soft and Crisp/Mechanical presets are original deterministic noise-and-tone synthesis in `src-tauri/src/core/input_sounds/library.rs`, also dedicated to CC0 1.0. Imported user audio is separate and retains its original rights.
