# Security

Please do not report vulnerabilities in public issues.

Email [mathis@screenix.studio](mailto:mathis@screenix.studio) with a description, the affected version (shown on `chrome://extensions`) and steps to reproduce. You will get a reply within a few days. Please give us a reasonable time to ship a fix before disclosing the issue.

In scope: this extension, and the parts of screenix.studio it talks to (license activation and refresh, share links). Out of scope: the debugger bar Chrome shows while recording, and pages Chrome does not let extensions record.
